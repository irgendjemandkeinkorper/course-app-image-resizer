Greensward / design system
Design system

The abstract feel of golf, made portable.

Greensward is a calm, premium, editorial system — early-morning light on open turf, precision and restraint — with none of the literal iconography. Warm sand neutrals, a deep matte fairway green, and a muted horizon blue. Semantic tokens sit over raw primitives, so re-theming for any brand is a one-file swap. Typography

Editorial serif display (Fraunces) over a quiet humanist sans (Inter). Modular ~1.2 scale, rem-based to respect user settings. Eyebrows recur as small tracked uppercase labels.
